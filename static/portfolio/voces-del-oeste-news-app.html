Project overview

Voces del Oeste is a news publication covering the west of the Dominican Republic and the wider world. We designed and published its mobile app, a free, Spanish-language news app available on Google Play and the App Store.

Business challenge

Readers increasingly follow the news on their phones. The publication needed a place on the home screen: an app that is quick to open, easy to read for every age group, and able to tell people when something important happens, on both Android and iOS.

Goals and requirements

  • Bring local, national and international coverage to phones
  • Organize stories by topic: politics, sports, culture and society
  • Send notifications for the most relevant news
  • Keep the design simple and easy to use
  • Publish on both Google Play and the App Store

Proposed solution

A clean news feed with clear topic sections, written for a Spanish-speaking audience, and a selective notification channel that brings readers back for the stories that matter. The same app was prepared, with its own listing, age rating and privacy declarations, for each of the two stores.

Implementation process

01

Plan the content structure

Sections, topics and news scopes (local, national, international) defined before design began.

02

Design a simple interface

A readable feed with topic sections and very little to learn, suitable for every age.

03

Build the feed and notifications

News feed, article view and push notifications for the most relevant stories.

04

Prepare and publish the store listings

Descriptions, screenshots, age ratings and privacy declarations for Google Play and the App Store.
