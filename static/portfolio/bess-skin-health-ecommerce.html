Project overview

Bess pairs topical creams and serums with oral supplements, an approach it calls inside-out. We designed and built the bilingual WordPress store around the shopper.

Business challenge

Shoppers needed to understand the inside-out idea, trust a health-adjacent brand and find the right product for their skin concern, without feeling overwhelmed. New customers needed reasons and ways to buy confidently.

Goals and requirements

  • Explain one idea across two kinds of product
  • Let people shop by concern, type, category or routine
  • Make credentials visible where decisions are made
  • Remove first-purchase friction

Proposed solution

The homepage leads with one headline and one button to the range, and a dedicated page explains how topical care and oral nutricosmetics work together. The menu offers four routes to a product, trust signals run through the site, and a slide-out cart, welcome offer, visible delivery and returns terms and ten payment options keep the path short.

Implementation process

01

Explain the idea in one glance

One clear headline and call to action, plus a page that explains the inside-out approach with cosmetics and supplements side by side.

02

Browse by what the skin needs

Menus by category, concern, product type and care protocol, with ready-made protocols as bundles.

03

Let the credentials do the talking

Formulation credentials, certification and sustainability badges, and a before-and-after slider.

04

Remove every reason to hesitate

Slide-out cart, clear sale pricing, welcome offer, visible delivery and returns terms and ten payment options.
