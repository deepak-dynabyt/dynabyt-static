A tidy portal and a sales process built for the field

An audit, cleanup and re-map of HubSpot for a staffing company: organized lists, reviewed workflows and a seven-stage pipeline built around how reps meet prospects.

Project overview

A company that supplies temporary workers to local businesses has reps who meet prospects in person, plus leads from a field-sales app and a contact-data provider. We audited HubSpot, cleaned it up and mapped a sales process that every rep can follow.

Client shown anonymously. Details shared with the client’s approval.

Business challenge

HubSpot held thousands of contacts, but records were incomplete, lists and workflows had piled up, and the steps from first contact to a signed agreement were not written down. Leads from the field-sales app and the data provider needed different first touches, and several workflows were unnamed, switched off, or turned on without actions.

Goals and requirements

  • See honestly what needed fixing first, and why
  • Remove clutter and keep what is useful
  • Write down how a lead becomes a customer, for both lead sources
  • Automate follow-up without automating judgment

Proposed solution

We reviewed ten areas of the portal and logged each finding with a recommendation and a priority. Auto-created and stale lists were removed or rebuilt as active lists, and a master exclusion list now keeps bounced, unsubscribed and invalid contacts out of mass emails. We mapped the journey from lead to signed agreement and built a seven-stage pipeline with custom properties for how the lead was met.

Implementation process

01

Start with an honest picture

Contacts, companies, calls, lists, snippets, ads, marketing email, landing pages, files and forms were reviewed. The audit set the order of the cleanup.

02

Remove the clutter, keep what is useful

Auto-created and stale static lists were removed, active lists replaced static ones where possible, and eleven empty, unnamed workflows were deleted.

03

Write down how a lead becomes a customer

A pre-sales path with a first response within days, a seven-stage deal pipeline, closed-lost reasons and a long-term pending stage.

04

Automate the follow-up, not the judgment

Workflows were mapped for first-touch sequences, task creation at each stage and long-term follow-up. Stage changes that need judgment stay manual.

A cleaner portal: fewer lists, no empty workflows and a master list for safe sending

A process on paper: two lead sources, one clear path from first contact to a signed agreement

Follow-up that does not depend on memory
