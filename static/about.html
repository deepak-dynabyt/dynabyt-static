About Dynabyt Technologies

We take responsibility for the systems behind your business.

Dynabyt Technologies is a technology consultancy for businesses whose CRM, website, tools and workflows have outgrown the way they were put together.

Why we exist

Most businesses do not have a technology problem. They have a connection problem.

A CRM is configured by one vendor, a website by another, an automation by a freelancer who has since moved on, and a mobile app by a fourth. Each piece works. Together they leave leads uncollected, data retyped and nobody sure who is responsible when something breaks.

Dynabyt Technologies was started to be the single, accountable team across that stack. Our work is built on hands-on CRM implementation and website development, extended into AI-assisted automation, integrations and mobile applications, so the layers are designed together instead of patched together.

We are deliberately practical. We would rather simplify a setup than add to it, say plainly when something is not feasible, and leave your team with documentation they can actually use.

How we work

Principles we build by

Business first

We start from your goals and processes, then choose technology. Never the other way around.

Documented, not mysterious

Properties, workflows and integrations are written down, so you are not dependent on us to understand them.

Honest about limits

We confirm feasibility before promising an integration and tell you when a simpler option is better.

Here after launch

Maintenance and improvement are part of the plan, because systems need care as the business changes.

Who we work with

Startups, growing businesses and established organizations

Our work spans education, healthcare, financial services, staffing, digital infrastructure and e-commerce. The details differ; the need for connected, dependable systems does not.

  • Teams adopting a CRM for the first timewho want it set up around their process from day one.
  • Teams whose CRM has become clutteredwith duplicate lists, silent workflows and unreliable reports.
  • Businesses with a website that needs to performand feed enquiries into the sales process.
  • Operations leaderswho want repetitive work automated, safely and visibly.

What we will not do

Straight talk

No guaranteed rankings or revenue, no invented case studies, and no pushing a platform because it pays us a commission. We recommend what fits.
