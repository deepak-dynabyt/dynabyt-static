Choosing an architecture

Native, hybrid or cross-platform?

Not every project needs two separate native apps. We recommend based on what you are building, not on what is easiest for us.

Native

Separate iOS and Android apps

Maximum control over platform features, performance and look and feel, with two codebases to build and maintain.

Best when: You need advanced device features, demanding graphics or performance, or platform-specific design.

Cross-platform

One codebase, two platforms

Shared code for iOS and Android with access to most device features, usually faster and more economical to maintain.

Best when: Most business, portal, booking and workflow apps, and MVPs that must reach both platforms quickly.

Hybrid / web-based

Web technology in an app shell

Reuse web skills and content inside an installable app for simpler requirements.

Best when: Content-led or form-based apps where deep native behavior is not essential, or as a first step before investing further.

Our recommendation comes with reasons. After discovery you receive a short architecture note covering the options, the trade-offs in cost, performance and maintenance, and the one we suggest.

FAQs

Questions we often hear

Do we need both an iOS and an Android app?

Not necessarily. Many projects are best served by a cross-platform build, and some start on a single platform. We recommend based on your users, requirements and budget.

Can you build an MVP first?

Yes. An MVP limits risk by focusing on the smallest version that tests your core idea. You can then extend it based on real feedback.

Can the app connect to our CRM or existing systems?

Yes, when the systems expose suitable APIs. We confirm what is possible, including authentication and limits, before design is finalized.

Who publishes the app to the app stores?

The app should be published under your own developer accounts so you own it. We guide you through setup and assist with submission.

What does maintenance involve?

Operating systems and devices change regularly, so apps need updates, bug fixes and monitoring. We offer maintenance plans so the app stays compatible and secure.

Do you have mobile apps in your portfolio?

Check the portfolio filter for Mobile Apps to see any app case studies we have published. We only show work we can verify, so if nothing is listed there yet, we are happy to talk through relevant experience and our approach on a call.

Have an app idea or a mobile workflow to build?

Tell us who it is for and what it needs to do. We will help you choose the smartest first version.
