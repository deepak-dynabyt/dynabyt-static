Choosing a stack

WordPress or custom PHP?

We recommend based on how your team will use the site, not on habit.

WordPress

A CMS your team can edit

A mature ecosystem, flexible content editing and a large pool of developers who can maintain it later.

Best when: Marketing sites, blogs, brochure and service sites, bilingual sites and stores where staff update content often.

Custom PHP

Built for a specific job

Lean, purpose-built code with only the features you need and full control over logic and data.

Best when: Web applications, portals, internal tools or integrations where a CMS would add weight without value.

Hybrid

Each in its place

A WordPress marketing site alongside a custom application or API service for the complex part.

Best when: You need easy content editing for the public site and bespoke logic behind it.

FAQs

Questions we often hear

Can you redesign our site without hurting our search rankings?

We audit the existing site first, keep valuable URLs and content, set up redirects where addresses change and check the result after launch. No one can guarantee rankings, but a careful migration avoids avoidable losses.

Will we be able to edit the site ourselves?

Yes. We set up the CMS so your team can update content without breaking layouts, and provide a short guide or training session.

Can the website connect to our CRM?

Yes. We connect forms, bookings and other touchpoints to HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce or another system, including source tracking and consent handling where required.

Do you build online stores?

We have built WordPress e-commerce sites. We will confirm the right platform for your catalog, shipping, tax and payment needs during discovery.

What about hosting, security and backups?

We can advise on hosting, configure backups and security, and offer maintenance plans for updates and monitoring.

Do you write the copy?

We can help structure and draft copy with your input. You know your business best, so we work from your material and review it with you.
