CRM & Revenue Operations

A CRM that reflects how your business actually operates.

We implement, clean up and optimize HubSpot, Salesforce and Zoho CRM so sales, marketing and service teams work from one reliable source of truth, with pipelines, automation and reports built around your process.

Why it matters

Clarity for the whole revenue team

Every record says what happens next

Pipelines, lifecycle stages and required properties make the next step obvious, so deals do not stall in the gaps.

Faster, more consistent follow-up

Lead routing, tasks and alerts happen automatically, so response time stops depending on who remembers.

Reporting you can trust

One data model and clear definitions mean the numbers in a dashboard match what the team sees in the CRM.

What we cover

From a first setup to a full revenue operations build

Start with one pipeline or rebuild the whole system. We scope the work to what your team needs now and leave room to grow.

HubSpot, Salesforce and Zoho CRM implementation

Platform setup configured around your sales process, users, teams and permissions.

Audits, cleanup, restructuring and migrations

Review lists, workflows, properties and data quality, then simplify what has piled up or move to a new platform.

Sales and marketing pipeline configuration

Stages, required fields, probabilities and closed reasons that match how deals really progress.

Contact, company, deal and custom property architecture

A clean data model with naming rules, so every report and workflow reads from the same fields.

Lifecycle stages and lead qualification

Sales and marketing automation

Workflow troubleshooting and optimization

Email sequences and templates

FAQs

Questions we often hear

Which CRM should we choose?

It depends on your sales process, team size, budget, existing tools and how much customization you need. We compare options against your requirements and give you a straight recommendation, including when the answer is to keep what you have.

Can you work with our existing CRM instead of starting over?

Yes. Many engagements begin with an audit. We keep what works, remove clutter, fix broken automation and rebuild only the parts that need it.

How long does an implementation take?

It ranges from a focused cleanup to a multi-phase build, depending on scope, data quality and how many people need to sign off decisions. We give a scoped plan and timeline after discovery rather than guessing up front.

Can you migrate data between CRMs?

Yes. We map fields, clean and de-duplicate records, run test imports and verify results before cutting over. What can be migrated depends on the export and import options of the systems involved.

Will our team be trained?

Yes. Training and documentation are part of the plan so that administrators and users understand how the system is set up and how to change it safely.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. You can continue with monthly maintenance, one-off improvement projects or ad hoc help when something breaks.

Ready for a CRM your team can rely on?

Tell us how your team sells today and where it gets stuck. We will suggest what to fix first.
